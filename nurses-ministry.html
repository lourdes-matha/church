Prayer and fellowship

Nurses Ministry

United in prayer, strengthened by the Word of God.

The Nurses Ministry brings nurses together in prayer and fellowship, supporting one another and remembering the needs of others. Through daily prayer, Scripture sharing and regular gatherings, members grow together in faith and service.

Daily activities

  • Divine Mercy Chaplet — every day at 3:00 PM on Zoom. Nurses from different regions lead the daily Chaplet, gathering together in prayer.
  • Daily Word of God. A daily Scripture post offers encouragement and an opportunity to reflect on God’s Word.

Weekly prayer meetings

  • International Nurses Prayer Meeting — Mondays at 8:30 PM on Zoom. Nurses from around the world gather for prayer and sharing the Word of God.
  • Canada Nurses Ministry Prayer Meeting — Thursdays at 8:30 PM on Zoom. A weekly gathering for prayer, followed by reflection and sharing on the Word of God by Fr. Daris.

Monthly and ongoing commitments

  • 24-Hour Chain Rosary (അഖണ്ഡ ജപമാല) — on the 13th of every month. Members join in a continuous 24-hour prayer chain dedicated to the Holy Rosary.
  • Unbroken monthly prayer. Individual nurses take turns leading prayer commitments, maintaining an unbroken chain of prayer throughout the month.
  • Holy Qurbana readings — once every two months. Nurses Ministry members serve the parish by proclaiming the readings during Holy Qurbana.
  • Intercessory prayers. Members offer ongoing intercessory prayers for specific requests and prayer needs, remembering those seeking prayerful support.
