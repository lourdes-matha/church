Daily activities

Every day at 3:00 PM · Zoom

Nurses from different regions lead the daily Chaplet, gathering together in prayer.

Daily Word of God

A daily Scripture post offers encouragement and an opportunity to reflect on God’s Word.

Weekly prayer meetings

International Nurses Prayer Meeting

Mondays at 8:30 PM · Zoom

Monthly and ongoing commitments

24-Hour Chain Rosary (അഖണ്ഡ ജപമാല)

On the 13th of every month

Members join in a continuous 24-hour prayer chain dedicated to the Holy Rosary.

Unbroken monthly prayer

Individual nurses take turns leading prayer commitments, maintaining an unbroken chain of prayer throughout the month.

Holy Qurbana readings

Once every two months

Nurses Ministry members serve the parish by proclaiming the readings during Holy Qurbana.

Intercessory prayers

Members offer ongoing intercessory prayers for specific requests and prayer needs, remembering those seeking prayerful support.
