Faith · Friendship · Celebration

An evening for our parish family.

Come together with Lourdes Matha for a joyful evening of music, dance, skits and Christmas cheer. From our youngest children to our grandparents, Parish Night celebrates the talents and bonds that make us a parish family.

As we prepare and celebrate together, may we grow in faith, strengthen friendships across our wards and share the joy of belonging to a community centred on Christ.
