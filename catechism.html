Our catechism school helps children know Jesus, understand their Catholic faith and grow in the spiritual traditions of the Syro-Malabar Church. In partnership with families, we encourage them to build a lasting relationship with God and live their faith with love and confidence.

Learning and living our faith

Catechism gives children a welcoming place to learn, ask questions and discover how faith connects with everyday life. Our focus includes:

  • Scripture and Catholic teaching: Understanding God’s Word, the teachings of the Church and the example of the saints.
  • Holy Qurbana and the sacraments: Helping children participate with understanding, reverence and devotion.
  • Prayer: Learning the prayers of the Church and developing a personal habit of speaking with God.
  • Christian living: Practising kindness, honesty, forgiveness and service at home, at school and in the community.

Rooted in our Syro-Malabar heritage

We help children appreciate the faith passed down through generations—our Holy Qurbana, liturgical traditions and spiritual heritage. As they grow up in Canada, we want them to feel connected to their Church and understand the place of these traditions in their lives.

Teachers and families working together

Around 30 teachers share their time, faith and care with our students. Through teaching, listening and encouragement, they help children feel supported in their journey with Christ.

Parents and guardians are essential partners. Family prayer, participation in Holy Qurbana, regular class attendance and conversations about what children are learning all strengthen the foundations of faith.

A place to belong

With more than 140 students, our catechism school is a vibrant part of Lourdes Matha’s parish family. Alongside learning, children build friendships and discover opportunities to participate and serve.

We want every child to know that they are loved by God, valued by their parish and welcomed as an important part of our community.
