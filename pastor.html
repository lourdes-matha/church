Our Pastor

Fr. Jaison Koonanickal

Fr. Jaison Koonanickal is from the Archdiocese of Tellicherry and was ordained a priest on December 30, 1996. He was born on October 2 in Keezpally, Kannur. He completed his seminary studies at St. Joseph’s Minor Seminary, Tellicherry, and St. Thomas Apostolic Major Seminary, Vadavathoor. He served at Infant Jesus Church, Ulickal; St. Augustine Church, Nellikutty; St. George Church, Kacherikkadavu; and several other churches.
