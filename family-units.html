Family Units

Family units are small faith communities that bring parish families together for prayer, fellowship and mutual support. Rooted in the Syro-Malabar tradition, they help families live the Gospel and stay connected to parish life. Each unit encourages participation in Holy Qurbana, faith formation, charitable service and parish celebrations. Unit coordinators help organize spiritual, catechetical, social and family-apostolate activities. Through these gatherings, families grow in faith, strengthen relationships and care for one another.
