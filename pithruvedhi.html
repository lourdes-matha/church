Family Apostolate

Pithruvedhi

In the Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, Pithruvedhi is the official association of the fathers of the parish. It provides a spiritual and social platform that encourages men to lead their families with faith, integrity and selfless love. Inspired by St. Joseph, its patron saint, Pithruvedhi helps fathers embrace their responsibility as protectors, providers and spiritual guides within their homes.

Members work closely with the Parish Priest and the wider parish community to support church activities and promote fellowship, service and responsible leadership. Through their example of faithful fatherhood, they help preserve the traditions of the Syro-Malabar Church and pass its values on to future generations.
