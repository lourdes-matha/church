Madhruvedhi

Welcome to the Madhruvedhi of our parish. Rooted in the rich traditions of the Syro-Malabar Church, Madhruvedhi is more than an organization—it is a spiritual sisterhood united in faith, prayer and service. Inspired by the humble “Yes” of our Blessed Mother Mary, we strive to strengthen our families in faith and serve as caring hands within our parish community.

Through prayer gatherings, parish activities and outreach to those in need, Madhruvedhi seeks to nurture the next generation in the love of Christ and keep the flame of faith alive in every home.
